- handleCardClick's click-set activeIndex deliberately untouched: the click expands immediately and the corrected observer then maintains/takes over/clears
- Women/Kids clicked state on the home page intentionally kept stable per the plan's documented known limitation (no section ids or hrefs exist for those routes yet; wiring them is separate feature work)
