# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
People shopping for considered, everyday clothing for themselves and their families — men, women, and kids — who want pieces that last, fit well, and don't require decoding a marketing stunt to buy. They browse on mobile and desktop, often during a break or while comparing, and they value clarity, honesty, and warmth over hype or spectacle.

## Product Purpose
Clothly is a curated fashion ecommerce. We exist to make buying well-made, well-fitting clothes for the whole family feel calm and considered — not a sale countdown. Success looks like a customer who comes back because the first piece fit, held up, and felt like them.

## Brand Personality
Considered. Warm. Intentional.
We speak like a trusted friend who happens to know clothes well — not a billboard, not a hype account. We earn trust through specifics (real materials, real makers, real fit) and through restraint (no countdown timers, no urgency theater, no "limited drop" posturing). The voice is declarative and quiet.

## Anti-references
- **Fast-fashion template look** (Shein / Temu / PLT): busy carousels, neon sale badges, countdown timers, gradient slogans, "BUY NOW" energy. Clothly is the opposite of this.
- **Streetwear-hype aesthetic** (Supreme / Off-White drop culture): black + bold red, drop counts, sneaker-hype, scarcity theater. We don't do scarcity.
- **Luxury-house sterility** (Hermès / Bottega): all-caps tracked headers, zero copy, cold minimalism, no human voice. We're warm.
- **Generic AI-minimal SaaS-cream** (the 2024–26 startup look): cream bg + Geist/Inter + generic hero + three icon tiles, indistinguishable from every AI landing. We are a specific brand with a specific voice, not a template.

## Design Principles
1. **Show, don't shout.** Let the pieces and the copy carry the weight. Decoration is earned, not assumed.
2. **Specifics over superlatives.** Real materials, real makers, real fit. "Crafted to last" beats "premium quality."
3. **Calm is a feature.** No countdown timers, no drop countdowns, no urgency theater. The site should feel like a good store, not a sale.
4. **One world, held with discipline.** Cream, ink, warm amber. Tonal depth, not many colors. Variation from layout and type, not from fill.
5. **The brand has a heartbeat.** Considered, not dead. Quiet motion, real micro-interactions, but never loud. Animated hovers and scroll-linked reveals only when they fit the piece.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA as a floor (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text and UI).
- Inputs at 16px on mobile to avoid iOS zoom.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animation.
- Color is never the only signal (icon + text for status, not color alone).
- Honest alt text for product imagery; real product photos, not stock.
